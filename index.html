Frequently asked questions.

What is an AI operating system?

Your own system in which all the AI models and tools you pay for run under one roof: a hub with the costs per model, a small assistant for every role and approval before anything is sent.

Where does our data run?

Wherever you want. Where client data must not leave the building, the models run on your server or your computer. No obligation to use the cloud.

How do we start?

With an audit: five days in which we look at how work really runs at your company. Then we build in weekly steps (4–8 weeks) and train your people.

Who owns the source code?

You do. For web apps and software we hand over source code, documentation and instructions.
