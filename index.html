how i work

Process / 3 phases

audit

I sit with your teams, map the real workflows, and find the places where AI removes cost or unlocks output. You get a written brief with numbers, not vibes.

Phase 02 — 4–8 weeks

I build the highest-value systems myself — automations, pipelines, control centres — in your stack, with your data, shipped in weekly increments you can veto.
