Example · Zulu drinks

20 terabytes of footage. One search box.

Zulu makes iced tea, cola and orange soda, and has been filming for years: 1,284 shoots on 20 TB of drives. We built them an AI that watches every clip and notes what is in it: products, people, places, light, mood. Now anyone in the team types what they need and gets the shot with its timecode.

  • All 20 TB indexed once, new shoots are added automatically
  • Search in plain words, in English or German
  • Runs on your own server, the footage stays in house

Frequently asked questions.

What kind of apps do you build?

Tools for the processes standard software doesn’t cover: searching a footage library by description, producing images that follow your brand guidelines, approvals, reports, data pipelines. Web, desktop or phone, connected to the tools you already use.

Where does our data run?

Wherever you want. Where client data must not leave the building, the models run on your server or your computer. No obligation to use the cloud.

How do we start?

With an audit: five days in which we look at how work really runs at your company. Then we build in weekly steps (4–8 weeks) and train your people.

Who owns the source code?

You do. For web apps and software we hand over source code, documentation and instructions.

What is an AI operating system?

The larger step, once several apps are running: your own system in which all the AI models and tools you pay for run under one roof, with a hub showing the costs per model, a small assistant for every role and approval before anything is sent.
