Example · Zulu drinks

20 terabytes of footage. One search box.

Zulu makes iced tea, cola and orange soda, and has been filming for years: 1,284 shoots on 20 TB of drives. We built them an AI that watches every clip and notes what is in it: products, people, places, light, mood. Now anyone in the team types what they need and gets the shot with its timecode.

  • All 20 TB indexed once, new shoots are added automatically
  • Search in plain words, in English or German
  • Runs on your own server, the footage stays in house
